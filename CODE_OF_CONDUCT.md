# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. We want a welcoming space for learners and contributors of every background and level.

Expected: patient, specific feedback; assuming good faith; crediting others' work; disagreeing about ideas without attacking people.

Not acceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, or posting
confidential material that belongs to someone else.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation that violate this code. To report a problem, use the
repository's private reporting options or contact the maintainer through their profile.
